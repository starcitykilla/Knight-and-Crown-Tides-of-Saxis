# Knight & Crown: Tides of Saxis

**A love story written in the tide.**

An open-world fantasy MMO set in a cursed version of Saxis, VA, where the real watermen families of the Chesapeake became magical bloodlines.

## Core Concept
On April 20, 2028, B.J. Knight and Shida B break The Sundered Vow on their wedding night and wake up 300 years in the past as Sir B.J. Guardian Knight and Queen Shida B Enchantress Sovereign. Players live their love story first, then wake to create their own character in the world they saved.

## The Three Bloodlines
1. **Tidewarden** - Tank/Guardian. House Knight. *“A Knight never leaves a boat tied loose.”*
2. **Star-Reader** - Healer/Tide Mage. House Crown. *“We tell the tide to recede.”*
3. **Marsh-Runner** - DPS/Rogue. Tide-Blood. *“Found, not born.”*

## Key Features
- **Mahlon's Lantern**: Real family heirloom becomes core mechanic
- **Vow Day April 20**: Annual server event — bay glows, all players water-walk
- **Crab Pot Housing**: Player housing = customizable crab boats
- **Dennis Shrine Respawns**: Faction spawns tied to real Saxis cemeteries

## Lore Foundation
Based on the true genealogy of the Knight family of Messongo/Saxis, VA:
- **Dunreath M. Knight** (c.1800-1845): Found overnight in Dun Reef Marsh. First Knight.
- **William Royal Knight** (1851-1925): Built House Knight. 
- **Harold H. Knight** (1891-1956): Waterman of Saxis.
- **Wayland (Mahlon) Knight I** (1915-1980): Keeper of Mahlon's Lantern.
- **Bradley Mahlon Knight Sr.** (Mahlon II): The Bridge.
- **Bradley Mahlon Knight Jr. - B.J.** (Mahlon III): The Heir. Breaks the curse.

House Motto: *Found in the Marsh. Forged by the Tide. Named for the Night.*
